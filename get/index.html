Dolla

The budget app for people paid every week. Know exactly what's safe to spend until your next paycheck.

Open & install

New accounts need an invite code from a friend who uses Dolla.

  • Safe to spend this weekOne clear number, based on your paycheck, bills and plan, that resets every week.
  • Plan every paycheckSplit each weekly check into bills, gas, food and savings in seconds.
  • Optional bank syncConnect your bank to bring in transactions automatically (limited spots), or just add spending by hand.

Install on Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap Open & install above in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen (or Install app).
  3. Open Dolla from your home screen and create your account with your invite code.
Free. Works offline. Your budget is saved, encrypted, to your account.
